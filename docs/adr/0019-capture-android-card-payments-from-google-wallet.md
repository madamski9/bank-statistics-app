# Capture Android card payments from Google Wallet

On iOS, card payments come from Apple Wallet notifications because only those name the merchant. Android will rely on Google Wallet in the same way: its notifications are the primary source of card payments, with the merchant, amount and currency of each purchase, while bank application notifications cover transfers, BLIK and other activity. Google Wallet joins the Allowlisted Source Apps as a wallet source, and its rules join the shared rule bundle and conformance corpus once real notifications have been collected and sanitized. A Google Wallet payment is captured only when its card can be attributed to a Supported Bank; payments with cards from other issuers are ignored with an explicit reason rather than assigned to the wrong bank. This keeps Android at parity with iOS without asking users to change how they pay.
